## Design Context

### Users
Students, researchers, and visitors of the cybersecurity laboratory use the site to browse and read cybersecurity lab news and articles.

### Brand Personality
Professional, editorial, and trustworthy. The interface should feel credible and focused without losing the character of a research and teaching laboratory.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the current dark editorial style, using the laboratory's CISUC branding and article photography as the visual anchors. The layout should feel calm, readable, and intentionally structured rather than generic or overly decorative.

### Design Principles
- Prioritize article readability and clear editorial hierarchy.
- Use the provided CISUC, article, and author imagery as meaningful identity cues.
- Keep content centered with comfortable, responsive spacing.
- Maintain strong contrast, visible keyboard focus, and reduced-motion support.
